About

I work on one question: how do people keep growing when the world changes faster than we can learn?

I design systems rather than courses: frameworks for identity, learning and leadership that people and organisations can use without me in the room.

Before that I spent more than 15 years in MarTech, CRM and customer experience at PepsiCo, Alibaba Group, Philip Morris International and Otto Group. Today I run an IT consulting company for B2B SaaS and lead projects on gender equality in tech. Based in Tashkent, working globally. I write more than I speak.
