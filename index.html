About

I work on one question: how do people keep growing when the world changes faster than we can learn?

I design systems rather than courses: frameworks for identity, learning and leadership that people and organisations can use without me in the room.

Today I combine more than ten roles across industries: technology consulting, education, research, international communities and leadership roles in global non-profit organisations.

Before that I spent more than 15 years in MarTech, CRM and customer experience at PepsiCo, Alibaba Group, Philip Morris International and Otto Group. Based in Tashkent, working globally. I write more than I speak.
