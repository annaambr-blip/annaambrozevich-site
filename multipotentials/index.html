From the author

My roles are not scattered. They are a system

For most of my life, I heard the same advice: choose one thing. One profession. One niche. One label.

I tried. For years, I tried to fit myself into a single shape. It never held.

Today I live across more than ten roles, in technology, education, communities and international work. And one quiet day, I finally saw it clearly: this was never a lack of focus. I am a multipotential. My roles are not scattered. They are a system.

That’s when this project began.

Multipotentials is a long-term research project, and its subject is me. I notice a tension, a rhythm or a pattern in my own life, I find a way to live inside it, and I share what works.

For now, this research lives in two places: a regular newsletter on Substack, and a book I’m writing right now. More will grow from it over time.

It’s not a guide written from the outside. It’s a system built and tested from the inside.

Anna Ambrozevich
