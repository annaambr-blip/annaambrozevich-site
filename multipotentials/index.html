Why I write it

A life of many roles needs a system

My whole life I kept hearing the same advice: choose one thing. One profession, one direction, one label. Today I hold more than ten roles, and I stopped apologising for it.

Multipotentials is where I share how I actually make this work: how I plan across roles, learn without burning out, decide what to let go and keep the whole system alive. Free, twice a month.

You’re not too many things. You just need a design that can hold all of them.
